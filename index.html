Campaign conversion

IPA files were already LevelXML. We stripped mobile-only ptm, sw, sh, r, cw from <info>.

User-created conversion

Weebly packs were .zip/.rar/.happywheels plists. We pulled the data LevelXML out, removed fm="m", turned 0/1 bools into f/t, and multiplied coordinates/sizes by 40 (iOS share scale → PC editor scale).

A few Weebly download buttons pointed at the wrong file or the same overwritten zip — duplicates were dropped. Levels that used the mobile-only Slow Motion Panel (t="5001") still contain that object; PC may ignore or choke on it.
